About

La La Cream is a modern skincare and bodycare brand focused on creativity, confidence, and making self-care more enjoyable.

-

Our Story
La La Cream was created by Brandon Brodeur, Elijah Suarez, and Jhovani !!!!!. We wanted to create a skincare brand that felt different from the typical companies while still having a clean and professional style.
Our products combine your everyday skincare with creative ideas to give customers something interesting to explore.

-

Our Mission
Our mission is to create creative skincare and bodycare products that make self-care easier than ever, to get you feeling like your a boss once again.

-

Meet the Founders
Founder - Elijah Suarez - focuses on the creative, content, and visual design of La la Cream
Co-founders - Brandon Brodeur - focuses on the developing of the website, coding, and fixing bugs to ensure a fully functional website for all.
Co-founders - Jhovani Aviles-Espinoza - focuses on website management, testing, and making sure the website is working properly.

-

Our Values
Creativity - We believe skincare should be personal, enjoyable, and a little different.
Confidence - We want our products and brand to encourage people to feel comfortable and confident.
Quality - we focus on creating a easy, and very understand description for our products, and ensure our products are fresh before shipped.
Customer Experience - We want our customers to have a great experience. We ensure everyone is equal.

-

Our Vision
Our vision with La la Cream was for our creative ideas to help people everywhere with whatever there need, at La la Cream we want to help everyone we can to make the world a better a shining place.
